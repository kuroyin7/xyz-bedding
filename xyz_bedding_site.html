Why It Is Made This Way

Luxury is what you feel, not what you're told.

Three decisions separate a sheet you tolerate from one you look forward to. We made all three the hard way.

01 — The Fibre

Long-staple cotton

Longer fibres mean fewer loose ends, a smoother surface, and a fabric that resists pilling for years rather than months. We source from mills that spin for the export houses.

02 — The Weave

A true sateen finish

A sateen weave floats more thread on the surface, giving that cool, faintly lustrous drape. Woven at 400 threads — high enough to feel substantial, low enough to still breathe.

03 — The Finish

Hemmed, not hurried

The details others cut — a clean mitred hem, tight even stitching, a considered fold — are where a sheet quietly announces its quality. We do not cut them.
